Cocktail

Sabrocito Mango Bellini

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Sabrocito Mango
  • 1 oz peach puree
  • 3 oz prosecco
  • mango slice, peach slice

Method

  1. Add the peach puree and Sabrocito Mango to a flute.
  2. Top with prosecco.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Garnish with a mango slice and a peach slice.
