Cocktail

Sabrocito Lychee Bellini

Ingredients

  • 2 oz Sabrocito Lychee
  • 1 oz peach puree
  • 3 oz prosecco
  • lychee fruit, peach slice

Method

  1. Add the peach puree and Sabrocito Lychee to a flute.
  2. Top with prosecco.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Garnish with a lychee fruit and a peach slice.
