Sabrocito Passion Fruit Sweet Chili Sauce

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp Sabrocito Passion Fruit
  • 1/4 cup sweet chili sauce
  • 1 tbsp passion fruit pulp
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp soy sauce.

Method

  1. Mix the sweet chili sauce, passion fruit pulp, lime juice, soy sauce, and Sabrocito Passion Fruit.
  2. Simmer for 2 minutes, stirring gently.
  3. Let the sauce cool completely.
  4. Serve as a dip for shrimp or spring rolls.
