Food

Sabrocito Reposado Honey Chipotle Enchiladas

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp Sabrocito Reposado
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • shredded chicken 2 cups
  • honey chipotle glaze
  • queso fresco
  • crema
  • sesame
  • rice

Method

  1. Mix the honey, chipotle, and Sabrocito Reposado into a glaze.
  2. Fill the corn tortillas with the shredded chicken.
  3. Roll the tortillas and cover them with the glaze.
  4. Bake for 15 minutes, then top with queso fresco and crema.
